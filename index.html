About

I have worked at the Institute of Artificial Intelligence at Ulm University since April 2021. My field is explainable AI and robotics, and my main topic is explainable robot navigation.

Before that I studied Electrical Engineering at the University of Sarajevo and Computer Science at Mälardalen University, from 2015 to 2020, with a focus on control engineering, robotics and computer vision.

My doctoral thesis has the title Explainable Robot Navigation: Grounding, Planning, and Adapting Explanations.

Research

A robot that moves among people makes decisions that those people cannot see: it stops, waits or takes another way. An explanation tells them why. Before a robot can give one, it has to settle what to explain, in which form, when, and to whom.

Put a chair in the robot's way: click or tap the floor. A second click takes the chair away.

Ask the robot

PersonWhy did you stop?

RobotA chair blocks my way to the shelf.

An illustration of the idea. The robot plans its way on this grid, and every answer is derived from that plan.

Five research topics

  1. Generation

    What to explain?

    How can XAI methods be applied and extended to generate intelligible, environment-grounded explanations?

    LIME on the local planner · spatial language · affordances

  2. Framework

    In which form?

    How can the explanation process be formalized, and which representation is best?

    Decision hierarchy HiXRoN · text, visual, or both

  3. Planning

    When, and for how long?

    How can the robot's profile and the context parameterize timing, duration, form and detail? How can scheduling be cast as planning?

    Robot personality · temporal planning

  4. Adaptation

    To whom?

    How can a robot learn and adapt its strategy to heterogeneous, uncertain preferences?

    Online learning · Thompson sampling

  5. Urgency

    And if there is no time?

    How can a robot communicate urgency in embodied interaction? Is it perceived as intended, effective and acceptable?

    Wording and pitch of the voice

Current work

  • Certified explanations

    Are you sure?

    How can the robot verify that the facts it names entail the cause?

  • Plan rationales

    Why this step?

    Which function does each action have in the robot's plan?

  • Timing under forgetting

    Why tell me now?

    When should an explanation end, if explaining takes time and people forget?
